Bundle layout
-------------
In the iOS app the license files named in ThirdPartyNotices.txt are bundled in
"Legal/licenses/", "EngineResources/licenses/" and
"PackageManager_PackageManager.bundle/RimePrelude/" (LICENSE is LGPL-3.0 and
COPYING is GPL-3.0; PROVENANCE.md records the source). HostApp links
ZIPFoundation and Yams (with its LibYAML) via PackageManager; the keyboard
extension does not.
